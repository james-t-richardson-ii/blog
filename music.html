Pre-Release · 2026

For most of my life, I have understood the world through patterns, logic, systems, and problems that could be broken down into smaller parts. That way of thinking has served me well. It has also become a place to hide.

These songs came from realizing that I had spent years becoming dependable, capable, productive, and useful while slowly losing contact with parts of myself that could not be measured on a dashboard or explained through a root-cause analysis.

Identity is not an album about technology, although technology is part of its language. The engineering metaphors are here because they are how I naturally make sense of the world. Protocols, rootkits, shortcuts, alerts, overrides, and broken processes become ways of talking about anxiety, relationships, exhaustion, communication, neurodivergence, responsibility, and the fear of becoming indistinguishable from the role you perform.

This album does not claim to answer those questions. It is an attempt to finally ask them honestly.

A Note on the Sound

Identity combines modern alternative rock, metalcore, rap rock, electronic textures, and moments of quieter vulnerability. The heavy sections represent overload, anger, pressure, and urgency. The rap-influenced sections provide space for dense ideas and internal monologue. The softer sections represent the moments after the alarms stop, when there is no volume left to hide behind.

Bass is especially important throughout. I do not only want music to be heard. I want it to be physically felt.

This is a pre-release version. These songs are being shared because I wanted them to exist outside of my own head before they were treated as finished products. Identity is about being seen more honestly, and that process cannot begin while everything remains hidden until it is perfect.
