# About James Richardson

I’m an engineering leader focused on platform engineering, infrastructure automation, reliability, security, and the operating models that help software organizations scale.

I’ve spent my career working across DevOps, SRE, developer platforms, source control modernization, regulated SaaS environments, distributed systems, and enterprise automation. My work tends to live at the intersection of technical depth and organizational change: building systems that make engineering teams faster, safer, and more consistent.

This site is where I write about the practical side of engineering leadership:

- how teams move away from click-ops
- how platform engineering changes developer productivity
- how infrastructure-as-code scales in real organizations
- how observability, security, and compliance become operating concerns
- how AI and automation are changing engineering operations
