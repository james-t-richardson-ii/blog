## What I'm making

**Music.** I just released the pre-release version of [*Identity*](/music/) — a six-song album I've been building for the past year. The process of finishing it turned out to be as meaningful as the music itself. There are things I could say in a song that I couldn't say any other way, and I wanted those things said before I talked myself out of it.

---

## What I'm working through

**Self identity and recovery.** The album wasn't only a creative project. It was part of something harder: trying to reconnect with parts of myself that got buried under years of being the person who handles things. The engineering metaphors in the songs are real — I genuinely think in systems — but they're also a way of describing something that has very little to do with infrastructure.

I'm still in that process. Making it visible felt like the honest next step.

---

## What I'm building at work

**Deeper observability with AI.** I'm spending serious time on what it actually means to move from "dashboards you stare at" to "systems that surface meaning." Not just smarter alerting — genuinely different signal. AI changes what's possible in this space and I'm working through what a real AI-native observability strategy looks like inside a large engineering organization.
