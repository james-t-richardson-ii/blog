A rootkit is designed to hide deep inside a system. It gains privileged access, alters what the system can trust, and becomes difficult to detect from within. That made it the right metaphor for intrusive thoughts.

Anxiety does not always feel like an external voice. It can feel like your own reasoning. Once it has established itself, every signal can look suspicious. Is this thought mine? Is this fear evidence? Is my mind warning me, or has the warning system itself been compromised?

This song uses paranoia and body-horror imagery to express the experience of not being able to fully trust your internal signals.
