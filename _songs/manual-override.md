The responsibilities are still being handled. The meetings are attended. The emails are answered. The problems are solved. From the outside, every diagnostic appears normal. But internally, something has stopped responding.

A manual override is necessary when the existing system cannot correct itself. In this song, that becomes a plea to interrupt the pattern before it becomes permanent. Continuing exactly as before is no longer an option.

At its heart, this is about wanting to feel again and not knowing how to begin.
