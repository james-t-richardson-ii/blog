I can solve complex technical problems. I can lead engineers, design systems, navigate incidents, and find failures buried several layers deep. But capability is not the same thing as ease.

As my career progressed, I moved farther away from the direct engineering work I loved and deeper into strategy, organizational problems, meetings, politics, and decisions made far from the systems themselves. I became responsible for the machine instead of working inside it.

The song is frustrated, aggressive, and intentionally conflicted. It is proud of what I built. It is angry about what the work consumed. The central tension is simple: I built the machine. Now the machine owns me.
